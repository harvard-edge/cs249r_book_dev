About MLPerf EDU

MLPerf EDU is an independent pedagogical ML systems benchmark preview developed alongside the Machine Learning Systems textbook. It draws methodological inspiration from MLCommons MLPerf but is not an official MLCommons benchmark and is not endorsed by MLCommons.

Design Philosophy

Authoritative workload inheritance. Each score-bearing definition starts from an established upstream model, dataset split, evaluator, and quality reference. The project contributes only the PyTorch adapter, measurement harness, quality gate, provenance, and reports needed for local execution.

Traceable lineage. Workloads identify their model, dataset, scenario, runner, and research lineage. Public-result candidates add structured asset dossiers and review packets.

Introspection for teaching. The harness records systems telemetry such as arithmetic intensity, data-loading behavior, and estimated energy when the platform supports it. Students can change controlled configurations without changing the benchmark’s workload identity.

Reviewable provenance. Runs emit reports and provenance manifests that bind results to source, assets, configuration, and environment evidence. Training and inference workloads can share checkpoint lineage when the registry declares that dependency.

Canonical and classroom paths. Registered workloads produce artifacts for the benchmark validation path. The five classroom labs (Labs 1–5) provide scaffolded educational experiments anchored in Wiggins & McTighe Backward Design, advancing from basic health checks to inference trade-offs, training ablations, multi-seed comparisons, and final assignment packaging. Lab 2 demonstrates the repository’s SUT_Interface locally.

Profiles as a Contract

min provides functional confidence. max runs the authoritative quality contract in its declared environment, and pro exposes the research envelope. A profile alone does not make a result publishable. Quality evidence, release validation, asset policy, and result wording must also pass the applicable gates. The running guide documents the current semantics.

Single Source of Truth

Every generated benchmark page comes from the workload registry, which is also consumed by the harness. The CLI reference comes from the CLI’s own help output. CI fails when native registry files, compatibility mirrors, review packets, or generated documentation drift.

Status and Governance

The repository contains the public-result rules, dataset release review, quality-target review, release checklist, and proposed MLCommons review path. The current external gates include expert target review, an authoritative component license for package publication, package-index publication, and MLCommons decisions about naming and result wording.

Development happens in the harvard-edge/cs249r_book repository. Feedback is welcome on the issue tracker.

Citing

The draft companion paper, MLPerf EDU: Bridging Industry Benchmarking and ML Systems Education, lives under paper/. It is not yet an archival benchmark citation. A stable citation will accompany a reviewed release.

Environment Variables
 

MLPerf EDU is an independent preview developed alongside the Machine Learning Systems textbook. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by MLCommons. Benchmark pages are generated from the workload registry.
